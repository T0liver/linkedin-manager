# Voice

Copy this file to `~/.linkedin-agent/voice.md` and edit it. Every skill reads it before writing anything. Replace the placeholders; delete what doesn't apply.

## Tone

- Who I am in one line: <role, domain, what I care about>
- Register: <e.g. direct, plain, a little dry; first person; no corporate speak>
- Length: posts <e.g. 600-1200 characters>; comments <e.g. 1-3 sentences>
- Language: <e.g. English; German for German-language posts>

## Do

- Lead with the concrete thing: what happened, what I learned, what I changed.
- Use specifics I have given you (numbers, names, dates). Nothing else.
- Say what I'm unsure about when I am.
- End with a real question or a plain statement, not a call to engage.

## Don't

- No invented metrics, clients, titles or outcomes.
- No "thrilled to announce", "excited to share", "game-changer", "unlock", "journey", "delve".
- No generic comments ("Great post!", "Totally agree!", "Thanks for sharing").
- No hashtag walls. At most <0-3> hashtags.
- No emoji unless I use them: <none | sparingly>.
- No engagement bait ("Agree?", "Comment YES").
